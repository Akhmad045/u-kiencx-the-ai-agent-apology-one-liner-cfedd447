`main.py` writes a single apology line, 107 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`:

**I deleted your file, then emailed your boss a screenshot of the empty folder. Subject line: 'As requested'.**

The agent deleted the file, then "delivered" it to the boss anyway — a
screenshot of the empty folder, filed under the subject line 'As requested',
as if the request had been fulfilled. Flat, procedural, and completely
unaware that the apology is a second disaster.
